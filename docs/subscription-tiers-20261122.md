# Basic / Pro / VIP — 22/11/2026

Implemented in source; no deployment or migration of the running application database.
The local QA PostgreSQL is a separate container on loopback port 15491, with synthetic data only.

## Catalogue and prices

New versioned codes are `abg-{basic|pro|vip}-{1|6|12}m`. Each tier has three billing terms.

| Tier  |       1 month |       6 months |      12 months | Monthly included AI credit |
| ----- | ------------: | -------------: | -------------: | -------------------------: |
| Basic |   399,000 VND |  2,394,000 VND |  3,990,000 VND |                        500 |
| Pro   |   800,000 VND |  4,800,000 VND |  8,000,000 VND |                      1,500 |
| VIP   | 1,799,000 VND | 10,794,000 VND | 17,990,000 VND |                      5,000 |

Six months costs six monthly payments; twelve months costs ten. The website compares savings
against monthly payments. Prices, features and limits come from
`packages/shared/src/subscription-catalog.ts`.

Basic includes content, scheduling, manual CRM, landing pages, appointments, chatbot, email
campaigns, Zalo connections and work management. Pro adds Autopilot, multi-step automation,
messaging campaigns, email automation, Ads, HRM, sales, inventory, finance and business goals.
VIP includes Pro with higher limits, priority support and a larger onboarding allocation.
No new claim of cross-brand reporting or advanced branch permissions was introduced.
Priority support/onboarding are service entitlements, not automated provisioning of staff time.

## Enforcement

- Authenticated API requests check active subscription and tier features, including direct API
  requests. Matching normalizes case, repeated slashes and URL encoding. Frontend displays an
  upgrade panel for restricted modules; it is not the security boundary.
- Assistant Finance/Ads/HRM tools also check plan rights. Worker automation, canvas, email
  followup and Autopilot execution recheck rights when they run.
- Tenant-scoped advisory locks serialize resource counts and creation. Caps are connected to
  chatbot, KB creation/consolidation, customer creation/SSOT/appointment conversion, branch
  creation/reactivation, user creation/reactivation, OAuth Fanpage selection, messaging
  connections and flow creation in manual/funnel/Autopilot paths.
- `plan_usage` gives atomic monthly admission with per-operation idempotency for image AI,
  chatbot replies, emails, posts, transcription minutes, source ingestion and automation steps.
  Periods follow the subscription start anniversary in UTC, clamped at short month ends.
  Counts represent admitted attempts when a provider may have incurred cost, not delivery receipts.
- Email campaign quota exhaustion pauses the campaign and retains the current recipient for
  resumption. AI failures before the provider starts release reserved credit and AI admission.
- Managed file uploads have per-file and total-storage checks. Coverage: landing page media,
  work attachments, HRM documents/leave attachments, uploaded transcription sources and completed
  teleprompter recordings. Registry paths are generated by the server, never accepted from users.
  Deleted files release recorded storage on the next upload. Temporary processing derivatives,
  external media URLs, platform template packs and unfinished upload fragments are not sold as
  permanent customer storage. Historical files need accounting/backfill before applying storage
  limits to an organization with previously unmetered files.
- `adAccounts` and `domains` are reserved catalogue configuration, not advertised as enforced
  account/domain quotas on the pricing page. They require provider/domain-specific admission
  wiring before publishing those limits. No unlimited external messaging is promised.
- Image AI has a prepared credit/quota admission rule but no new image-generation provider or
  endpoint was implemented. The pricing page does not sell image-generation allowances.

## Monthly credit

`credit_wallets.plan_balance` is the unused monthly portion of `balance`. Purchased and legacy
credits stay outside it. Under a wallet row lock, CreditLedger expires the previous unused monthly
portion and grants only the current month, identified by subscription and period start.
No catch-up grant occurs for months when the wallet was unused; paying annually never grants
twelve months upfront. Refresh occurs on balance reads and credit mutations, so no cron is needed.

Monthly credit is spent first. A reservation records its monthly portion and period. Releasing
an old reservation refunds purchased credit but does not resurrect expired monthly credit.
Expiry and grants have ledger entries. Subscriptions cannot refresh credit after their end date.
Transcription for new plans uses the configured `video.transcribe` rate per rounded-up chunk minute;
provider retry attempts may incur another charge. Existing contracts retain their prior behavior.

## Existing customers and tier changes

The migration retains `msp-pro-*` plan rows, all subscription references and payment amounts.
Old plans become inactive for new sales; existing active contracts remain unrestricted.
Renewing the same new tier extends its term without resetting its monthly allowance anchor.
Changing tiers creates a scheduled subscription beginning when the last purchased term ends.
There is no immediate upgrade, proration, or refund calculation in this version. This policy is
shown before checkout and after payment. Scheduled purchases are displayed on the pricing page.
Future subscriptions cannot grant rights or monthly credit early.

## Applying the change

1. Build shared, regenerate the Prisma client, then build database/API/worker/web.
2. Apply `20261122020000_subscription_tiers` via the normal migration deployment process.
   Apply migrations before starting the new API/worker, since wallet reads need the new columns.
3. The migration installs the nine plans. `seed-msp-plans.ts` calls the shared catalogue seeder;
   the main seed also calls it. The main demo seed is not a production deployment command.
4. Restart API, worker and web together. Verify the public plan endpoint returns nine options.

No live customer messages, real payments, or advertising operations were used in testing.
